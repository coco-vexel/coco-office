# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Coco Office a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing technical discussion on observable behavior and evidence.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from the
  experience.
- Prioritizing what is best for the overall community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Temporary or permanent
participation restrictions may be applied when necessary.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's private
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form and prefix the report title with
`[Conduct]`. This is a confidential intake channel and does not imply that the
report concerns a software vulnerability. Maintainers will respect the privacy
and safety of the reporter.
